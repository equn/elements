The song is played live in your browser — no audio file — using a patter melody in the style of “I Am the Very Model of a Modern Major-General” (Arthur Sullivan & W.S. Gilbert, The Pirates of Penzance, 1879), which is in the public domain. The tradition of singing the elements to that tune comes from Tom Lehrer’s “The Elements” (1959); Lehrer placed his lyrics in the public domain in 2020 and his recordings in 2022. This version sings all 118 elements in atomic-number order, so the table lights up left to right — Lehrer’s original stops at 102 and runs in rhyme order.

Chinese: Simplified characters (简体). Elements 104–118 use very new, rare characters that many fonts cannot draw, so those show their pinyin instead.
